1. Scope and summary

This Privacy Policy explains how SSForge handles information when you use SATOLITO, a local-first personal budgeting application. SATOLITO does not require an account and does not operate a developer backend or cloud synchronization service for your financial data.

Your financial records are processed on your device. SSForge does not receive your account names, transactions, balances, plans, reconciliations, descriptions, backup passwords, backup files, or CSV exports through the app.

2. Information processed on your device

SATOLITO may process and store information you enter or create, including:

  • cash, bank and credit-card display names and account types;
  • opening balances, income, expenses, transfers, card payments and refunds;
  • amounts, transaction dates, categories, income sources and optional descriptions;
  • plans, expected income and expenses, recurring templates and their completion status;
  • reconciliation observations, explanations and immutable correction history;
  • base currency, language, appearance, onboarding and other app preferences.

SATOLITO uses this information only to provide budgeting, double-entry financial history, account balances, planning, reconciliation, guidance and financial-summary features on your device.

3. Local storage and security

SATOLITO stores its working data in a SQLite database inside the app's private Android storage. Android application sandbox protections restrict ordinary access by other apps. The SQLite database is not independently encrypted; device-level security, screen lock and storage encryption remain important.

The app disables Android-managed automatic cloud backup and supplies exclusion rules for legacy backup and Android 12+ cloud-backup and device-to-device transfer paths. Android and device manufacturers ultimately implement those platform mechanisms, so no application can guarantee exclusion from every undocumented manufacturer-specific transfer path.

4. User-controlled encrypted backup and restore

When you explicitly create a SATOLITO backup, the app packages your local data into a .satolito file protected with AES-256-GCM encryption. A key is derived from the password you enter using Argon2id. The password and derived key are processed locally and are not sent to SSForge.

SSForge cannot recover a forgotten backup password. A restore first authenticates and validates the selected backup. After your confirmation, a successful restore fully replaces the current SATOLITO state; it does not merge old and new records.

You choose where to save or retrieve the backup through Android's document picker. If you choose a cloud or third-party storage provider, that provider's privacy and security terms apply to the file after you place it there.

5. Unencrypted CSV export

SATOLITO can create a readable CSV export of financial history only after showing a warning and receiving your confirmation. CSV exports are not encrypted. You should save them only in a trusted location and take care when sharing them.

You choose the destination through Android's document picker. SSForge does not receive the exported file. The selected storage provider may process or synchronize it under its own terms.

6. Accounts, backend services and third-party SDKs

SATOLITO currently:

  • does not create user accounts;
  • does not operate a developer backend or cloud synchronization service;
  • does not include advertising SDKs;
  • does not include usage-analytics SDKs;
  • does not include crash-reporting SDKs; and
  • does not sell personal or financial information.

Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer and any storage provider you select may process information under their own policies as platform or provider services. SATOLITO does not embed those services as advertising, analytics or crash-reporting integrations.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local SATOLITO data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or replace the current state by restoring a backup. SATOLITO preserves an auditable local financial history: edits and cancellations use correction records rather than silently rewriting finalized entries, and archived accounts retain their history.

Backups and CSV files saved outside the app remain wherever you placed them until you or the selected storage provider delete them. Clearing SATOLITO's app storage or uninstalling SATOLITO does not necessarily delete those external files.

Because SATOLITO has no user accounts or developer-hosted financial database, SSForge has no remote SATOLITO account or financial dataset to delete. You can contact us regarding support messages or other information you have sent directly to SSForge.

8. Support communications

If you contact SSForge by email, we receive the email address, message content and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request, investigate issues and meet applicable legal or security obligations. Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and is not combined with SATOLITO's local financial database.

Do not send backup passwords, decrypted backups, unencrypted financial exports or other unnecessary sensitive financial information in a support message.

9. Your choices and responsibilities

  • Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and device security settings.
  • Use a strong, unique backup password and keep it separately in a safe place.
  • Control and delete backup and CSV files using the storage provider where you saved them.
  • Clear SATOLITO app storage or uninstall the app if you want to remove its local working data from the device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if SATOLITO's features, data practices, legal requirements or third-party components change. We will update the effective date above and, when appropriate, provide notice in the app or store listing. Material data-practice changes will be reflected before the related feature is released.
